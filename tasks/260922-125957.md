## Prerequisites knowledge to the reader

-  Graphs theory
  - Euler Characteristic $\chi = V - E + F$
- Complex numbers
  - "the reader should be comfortable handling complex numbers and have a visual understanding of them"
  - Complex conjugates
  - Isomorphism $C \leftrightarrow \mathbb{R}^2$
  - Euler Formula $e^{\theta i} = cos(\theta) + sin(\theta) i$
- Linear algebra
  - Familiarity with the concept of a projection
  - Isometry
